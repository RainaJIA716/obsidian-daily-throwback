## 短描述（自动从 manifest 带入）
Show the same day from every earlier year at the top of a daily note, as folding cards with the full entry inside. Never edits your notes.

## 长描述
Daily Throwback puts the same day from every earlier year at the top of your daily note. Add one empty code block to your daily template and each note shows the entries for its month and day from all previous years, newest first: 1 year ago, 3 years ago, 7 years ago. Missing years are skipped, and entries you backfill later appear on their own.

Each year is a folding card. Cards start folded, so the block stays a few lines tall and you keep writing below it; an entry is only read when you open its card, then renders in full with images, links and tags. Long entries scroll inside the card.

Daily notes are found by file name (YYYY-MM-DD by default, configurable) in any folder, with an optional folder limit. A command adds the block to notes written before.

The plugin never edits your notes: cards are drawn on screen only, and an old entry's own block is left out so cards never nest. Works on desktop and mobile; the interface follows Obsidian's language.
